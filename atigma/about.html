Emma Atigma Karmasun, Founder

Emma Atigma Karmasun

Founder & Creative Director

Emma grew up in her mother's sewing shop, where early exposure to garment construction sparked a lifelong passion for fashion. She went on to study English Education at university, but always knew her true path lay in design.

During national service, she saved her monthly allowance to fund the beginning of Atigma. Afterwards she rented a small one-bedroom apartment, living and working there with her first industrial sewing machine, while enrolling in fashion school to sharpen her technical skills — graduating in 2021.

In the years since, Emma has worked independently, creating custom garments and reinvesting every profit back into the business — acquiring more machines, moving into a larger studio, and building a small team of women who now work alongside her.

Today, Atigma is a growing studio producing bespoke garments while gradually transitioning into ready-to-wear collections, with a vision to expand the team and one day train young girls in sewing and independent livelihoods.

Brand overview

Atigma is a contemporary Ghanaian womenswear brand crafting elegant made-to-order and bespoke apparel for the modern African woman. Rooted in structure, sophistication, and intentional design, Atigma creates pieces that celebrate real women — petite, tall, slim, and full-figured — with a strong focus on corporate and lifestyle dressing.

Brand identity

Atigma stands for strength, refinement, and quiet confidence. Each garment is thoughtfully designed to flatter the body, enhance movement, and transition effortlessly from day to night. Clean lines, precision tailoring, and timeless colour stories define every piece.

Design philosophy

Fashion should empower, not exclude.

Atigma merges classic tailoring with contemporary African femininity, creating clothing that supports a woman's ambition, self-expression, and daily rhythm. Every design prioritises fit, function, and fabric, ensuring comfort, longevity, and effortless style.

Who she designs for

The Atigma woman is a modern professional navigating corporate, creative, or entrepreneurial spaces. She is career-driven yet style-conscious, intentional about quality, fit, and individuality — frustrated by ill-fitting fast fashion and uninspired workwear, and willing to invest in fewer, high-quality pieces. She's drawn to Afro-modern aesthetics blended with global elegance: bold yet graceful, smart yet soft, feminine yet powerful.

Signature style

Atigma designs blend modern elegance with expressive femininity. Signature elements include structured waistlines, bold sleeves, flared silhouettes, and confident tailoring — from wide-legged palazzo pants to crisp cropped shirts. Each piece balances strength and softness, offering garments that flatter, empower, and elevate the wearer, whether she's owning the room or owning her moment.
